# Research Paper

## Working Title

Spectral and Geospatial Characterization of Lonar Crater as a Terrestrial Analogue for the Ancient Martian Lake Environment of Jezero Crater

## Research Question

Can multispectral reflectance characteristics of Lonar Crater reveal mineralogical and environmental patterns comparable to those observed in the ancient Martian lake environment of Jezero Crater, and what are the limitations of using such spectral similarities to interpret potential biosignatures?

## Research Structure

1. Title and Research Question
2. Abstract
3. Introduction and Scientific Background
4. Objectives and Hypothesis
5. Materials and Methodology
6. Results
7. Discussion
8. Limitations and Future Scope
9. Conclusion
10. References

The manuscript will document the scientific background, computational workflow, results, interpretation, limitations, and future research directions.
